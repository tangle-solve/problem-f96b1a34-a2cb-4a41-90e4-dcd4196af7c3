### CHEM-000519: Dense-cloud sulfur budget and phase partition
- **Subfield:** Astrochemistry
- **Open:** Quantify sulfur partition among gas, salts, polysulfanes, elemental sulfur and refractory grains in the same sightlines, explaining depletion through cloud evolution.
- **Status:** The decades-old budget problem has plausible candidates: 2025 experiments produced polysulfanes and S₈ in irradiated ice. XRISM iron-sulfide evidence from other sightlines does not close every dense-cloud budget.
- **Type:** experimental | theory
- **Why:** The reservoir’s volatility determines sulfur delivery to planets.
- **Key works:** A. Herath et al. (2025), *Missing interstellar sulfur in inventories of polysulfanes and molecular octasulfur crowns*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-025-61259-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61259-2); L. Corrales et al. (2025), *XRISM insights for interstellar sulfur*, [DOI:10.1093/pasj/psaf068](https://doi.org/10.1093/pasj/psaf068).
